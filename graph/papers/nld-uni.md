# Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion-Uni: Elastic Self-Refining Unified Diffusion LLM with Cross-Paradigm On-Policy Distillation

## Abstract Summary
NLD-Uni is a unified discrete diffusion LLM. Cross-paradigm OPD transfers AR VLM understanding and flow generation. Progressive self-refining corruption lets inference commit tokens before the final unmask.

## Key Contributions
1. NLD-Uni as an active unified-diffusion OPD plug-in on task:diffusion-lm-ar-delta-recycle.

## Empirical Highlights
Cross-paradigm OPD plus progressive self-refining vs absorbing-mask unified diffusion. Does not retarget method:a2d or method:opd.
