# Guided-RLVR: Guided Exploration in Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

## Abstract Summary
RLVR rollouts often collapse onto a narrow set of reasoning trajectories. Guided-RLVR injects steering tokens into the sampling distribution at critical thinking steps to expand exploration.

## Key Contributions
1. Guided-RLVR as an active exploration plug-in on task:math-code-rl-dense.

## Empirical Highlights
Outperforms existing RLVR methods on math and code while increasing trajectory diversity. Does not retarget method:cispo.
