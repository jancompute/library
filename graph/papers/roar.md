# Improving Reasoning with Rollout Agreement for Group-Based Policy Optimization

## Abstract Summary
Correct GRPO rollouts still differ in spurious reasoning. Blind pairs: low-agreement correct traces have more inconsistencies (35% vs 22%). RoAR adds a reasoning-agreement reward on verified-correct rollouts.

## Key Contributions
1. RoAR as an active plug-in on task:math-code-rl-dense.

## Empirical Highlights
Agreement among verified-correct traces is the extra signal. Does not retarget method:cispo.
