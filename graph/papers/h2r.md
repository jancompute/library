# H^2R: Hierarchical Hint-Based Reward for Mathematical Reasoning via Reinforcement Learning

## Abstract Summary
H2R uses the sub-question tree of a math problem as intermediate RLVR conditioning. Hierarchical hints are logically grounded milestones that shorten the remaining horizon to the final answer, unlike chain-of-thought prompts. HiMath is the accompanying structured benchmark.

## Key Contributions
1. Hierarchical hint-conditioned RLVR for multi-step math.

## Empirical Highlights
Hints help even without task-specific training; HiMath released as the eval. No public code. Does not retarget method:cispo.
