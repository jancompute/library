# When and How Does Muon Precondition? The Role of Structured Gradient Noise

## Abstract Summary
Under isotropic gradient noise Muon is just a learning-rate rescale. Preconditioning gains appear when gradient noise is structured.

## Key Contributions
1. Structured-noise condition for Muon preconditioning; not a train kernel.

## Empirical Highlights
- Does not retarget Muon2. Isotropic-noise theory is the wrong model for the Muon gain.
