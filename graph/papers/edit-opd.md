# Edit, Don't Imitate

## Abstract Summary
Visual math can fail before reasoning starts when a VLM misreads a diagram. Edit-OPD applies minimal on-policy edits instead of imitating a full teacher trace.

## Key Contributions
1. Edit-OPD as an active plug-in on task:student-distillation.

## Empirical Highlights
- Minimal-edit on-policy distill for VLM math. Does not retarget method:opd.
